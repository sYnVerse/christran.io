<p>
	<strong>illusYn</strong> is an advanced AI art studio that translates text descriptions and input images into high-fidelity visuals. Powered by Cloudflare's serverless AI inference registry, it provides content creators and designers with a full-featured workspace to generate, edit, crop, and isolate image elements instantly in their browsers.
</p>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Text-to-Image Generation</strong> — Compiles complex textual prompts into detailed digital paintings, vector assets, and illustrations.</li>
	<li><strong>Image-to-Image Redefinition</strong> — Allows users to upload baseline sketches or photographs and apply prompt styles to iterate on concepts while preserving structural integrity.</li>
	<li><strong>AI-Powered Background Removal</strong> — A premium inference tool that isolates objects using the Segment Anything Model (SAM), executing edge-based background subtraction in seconds.</li>
	<li><strong>Dynamic Canvas Cropping</strong> — An interactive in-browser cropping tool that lets creators adjust aspect ratios, isolate focal points, and re-encode outputs.</li>
</ul>
