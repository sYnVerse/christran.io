<p>
	This lab started with a decommissioned Dell PowerEdge R730 server generously donated by Auto-Chlor System. 
	I rescued this hardware from a future at an e-waste facility and turned it into the foundation of a dedicated, 
	enterprise-grade testing environment. Since then, it has grown into a multi-host setup that allows me to 
	simulate production-level infrastructure at home.
</p>

<h2>What It Runs</h2>

<p>
	The lab serves as a sandbox for building, breaking, and rebuilding infrastructure without impacting 
	production environments. Day-to-day, it supports:
</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Virtualized infrastructure</strong> — running multiple VMs and containers to mirror enterprise topologies</li>
	<li><strong>Network simulation</strong> — testing complex routing, switching, firewall, and VPN configurations</li>
	<li><strong>Security testing</strong> — practicing hardening, monitoring, and incident response workflows</li>
	<li><strong>Automation development</strong> — prototyping Ansible playbooks, Python scripts, and CI/CD pipelines</li>
	<li><strong>Continuous learning</strong> — preparing for certifications and experimenting with new technologies</li>
</ul>
