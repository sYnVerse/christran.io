<p>
	<strong>sYnX</strong> is a fast, collaborative to-do list app for organizing and tracking personal and professional tasks on the web and on Android. The web app is built with the Remix framework and runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database. The Android app is built with Expo and React Native and uses the same account and data through the web app's REST API. Sign-in goes through <a href="/portfolio/synid">sYnID</a>, the sYnVerse single sign-on server.
</p>

<h2>Core Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Collaborative Sharing</strong> — Share task lists as Private, View-Only, or Collaborative. Lists opened from someone else's link appear under "Shared with me" with the owner's display name, and emails are never shown.</li>
	<li><strong>Nested Subtasks</strong> — Break tasks down into subtasks nested under their parent task.</li>
	<li><strong>Priorities, Durations & Categories</strong> — Give tasks a priority and an optional duration, and organize lists by category (Checklist, Study, Shopping, Work, Travel, and more, or a custom one).</li>
	<li><strong>Reminders & Recurring Tasks</strong> — Set reminders with quick presets or a picked date and time, and repeat tasks Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. Repeating tasks keep their local time in your home time zone, even across daylight saving changes.</li>
	<li><strong>Calendar View</strong> — See tasks on a calendar next to the list view. On the web, drag unscheduled tasks onto a day.</li>
	<li><strong>Search & Sorting Options</strong> — Search tasks (including subtasks), filter by All, Active, or Done, and sort by Date Created, Priority, Reminder Time, or Name.</li>
	<li><strong>Multi-Channel Notifications</strong> — An every-minute cron sends due reminders by email, text message, Discord webhook, and push notification to your phones, with browser notifications while the web app is open.</li>
	<li><strong>Single Sign-On & Guest Mode</strong> — Sign in once with sYnID, with sessions that renew in the background. Without signing in, the web app works in guest mode: guest lists stay in the browser and move to your account when you sign in.</li>
	<li><strong>Android App & Widgets</strong> — A native Android app with Tasks, Calendar, Lists, and Settings tabs, three home screen widgets (check off tasks, see what's up next, track progress and quick add), deep links, an offline copy of your lists, and optional sync of reminders to a device calendar.</li>
	<li><strong>Theme Customizer</strong> — Choose between Midnight Indigo, Emerald Forest, Sunset Rose, Cyberpunk Neon, and Light Pastel themes on the web, and light, dark, or system themes on Android.</li>
</ul>
