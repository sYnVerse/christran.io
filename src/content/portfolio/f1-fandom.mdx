<p>
	<strong>F1 Wiki Automator</strong> is a comprehensive automation pipeline that keeps the Formula 1 Wiki on Fandom updated with real-time race data. By pulling from open F1 telemetry endpoints and parsing session times, it formats, compiles, and deploys MediaWiki wikitext templates automatically at the conclusion of every Grand Prix weekend session.
</p>

<h2>Core Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>21 Career Stats Templates</strong> — Computes cumulative stats (Championships, Wins, Podiums, Poles, Entries, Laps Led, etc.) by blending historical baselines with active season results.</li>
	<li><strong>Practice Timing Scraping</strong> — Scrapes raw practice tables from Formula1.com and formats the complex time gaps and sector splits into clean MediaWiki markup.</li>
	<li><strong>Smart Caching</strong> — Stores session payloads inside Cloudflare KV to prevent redundant external API calls and stay within free tier rate limits.</li>
	<li><strong>Admin Operations Reports</strong> — Integrates with Resend to email a daily digest detailing bot attempts, edits made, API quotas, and failures (at 6 AM PST).</li>
	<li><strong>Turnstile Integration</strong> — Protects the web dashboard's manually triggered updates using a Turnstile validation check.</li>
</ul>
