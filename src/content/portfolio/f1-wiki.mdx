<p>
	The <strong>Formula 1 Wiki</strong> on Fandom is a comprehensive community-driven encyclopedia documenting F1 history, statistics, results, and profiles. As an administrator and technical contributor, I oversee day-to-day community operations, enforce guidelines, and architect the site's template infrastructure to display complex stats in clean, responsive tables.

<h2>Key Responsibilities</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Content Curation & Enforcement</strong> — Defining writing standards, reviewing edits, and maintaining historical Grand Prix accuracy.</li>
	<li><strong>Template Engineering</strong> — Creating reusable wikitext templates to handle repetitive data tables (e.g. driver career records, qualification timing grids).</li>
	<li><strong>Visual Styling</strong> — Writing global CSS overrides on Fandom to modernize table designs, improve dark-mode visibility, and adapt layouts for mobile devices.</li>
	<li><strong>API Bot Coordination</strong> — Designing guidelines for automated script bots (such as the F1 Wiki Automator) to deploy statistics updates without disrupting regular editor flows.</li>
</ul>
