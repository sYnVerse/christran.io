## A New Era Begins

The 2026 Formula 1 season marks the beginning of a revolutionary era for the sport, characterized by the most significant regulation overhaul in over a decade. These changes aim to improve racing, enhance sustainability, and attract new manufacturers. The grid has transformed, the cars are nimbler, and the engines are greener.

## 2026 Regulation Changes

The 2026 ruleset focuses on a reimagined power unit and a "nimble car" concept, shifting the sport towards a future that balances road-relevance with pure racing spectacle.

### Power Unit Overhaul
* **50/50 Power Split:** The 1.6-liter V6 turbo internal combustion engine (ICE) remains, but the power distribution has changed drastically. The electrical power output has nearly tripled (from ~120kW to **350kW**), creating an even 50/50 split between the ICE and the electric motor.
* **Removal of MGU-H:** The complex Motor Generator Unit-Heat (MGU-H) has been eliminated to reduce costs and attract new manufacturers.
* **100% Sustainable Fuels:** All power units will now run on "drop-in" advanced sustainable fuels, ensuring no new fossil carbon is released into the atmosphere.

### Aerodynamics & Chassis ("Nimble Car" Concept)
* **Active Aerodynamics:** Traditional DRS (Drag Reduction System) is replaced by active aero elements on the front and rear wings.
    * **Z-Mode:** High downforce configuration for cornering.
    * **X-Mode:** Low drag configuration for straights to maximize top speed.
* **Manual Override Mode:** Instead of DRS for overtaking, drivers now have a **"Manual Override"** boost system. When close to a car ahead, the chasing driver can deploy extra electrical energy to attack.
* **Smaller & Lighter:** Cars are designed to be more agile, with a 30kg weight reduction (minimum weight 768kg) and smaller dimensions (shorter wheelbase and narrower track).

## The Expanded Grid: New Teams & Manufacturers

The 2026 grid expands to **11 teams** and features a reshuffled driver market, including high-profile transfers and the arrival of rookie talents.

### Manufacturer Entrants
* **Audi F1 Team:** Taking over the Sauber entry, Audi enters as a full works team with its own power unit.
* **Cadillac Formula 1 Team:** The American giant joins as the **11th team** on the grid. Initially utilizing Ferrari customer engines, they are fielding an experienced lineup.
* **Ford:** Returns to F1 as a technical partner to Red Bull Powertrains.
* **Honda:** Returns officially as the works power unit supplier for **Aston Martin**.
