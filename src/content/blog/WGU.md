The **[Accelerated BS/MS Program in IT](https://www.wgu.edu/online-it-degrees/accelerated-information-technology-bachelors-masters-program.html)** from **Western Governors University (WGU)** allows students to earn both a **bachelor's degree in IT** and a **master's in IT management** in a streamlined manner. The program is **fully online**, making it ideal for professionals looking to **balance work, education, and personal life**.

## 🚀 Why Choose This Program?

This **self-paced program** lets students progress based on their knowledge and experience. Some key benefits:

- **Complete Two Degrees Faster**: Earn a **BSIT and MSITM** in about **four years**, depending on your pace.
- **Industry Certifications Included**: Gain relevant **IT certifications** during your coursework.
- **Flexible Learning**: Study online anytime, anywhere—perfect for working professionals.
- **Build Leadership Skills**: The **master's portion** focuses on **IT management**, helping graduates step into leadership roles.

## 📚 What You'll Learn

The curriculum covers **foundational IT knowledge** as well as **management and strategy**, including:

- **Networking & Security**
- **Programming & Scripting**
- **Data Management**
- **Systems & Services**
- **IT Leadership & Strategy**

## 💰 Cost & Time Efficiency

Tuition is **charged per six-month term** rather than per credit hour, meaning students can complete courses **at their own pace** without paying extra for additional credits.

## 🛠 My Experience & Tips

Overall, the program is great for me since it's **online/remote**, allowing me to continue working my **full-time IT job** while carving out time for classes. One of the best aspects is the **huge online community** of current students and alumni who actively share tips and resources.

My primary sources of support have been **Reddit and Discord**:
- On **Reddit**, simply searching for the **course number** (e.g., `D325 WGU`) reveals a wealth of posts sharing **experiences and advice**.
- On **Discord**, there's an **official WGU server**, but I found the **"Unofficial WGU" Discord** more useful. You can join here: [Unofficial WGU Discord](https://discord.gg/unwgu).

Special **shoutout to Yukie** for her **detailed study guides**! She's documenting her journey through the **BSIT to MSITM program**—you can check out her resources here: [Yukie's Study Guide Index](https://yukietwilightnakama.notion.site/Yukie-s-WGU-Study-Guide-Index-449b9f08923846a4b5d0281a8d5e6b24).

## ⏳ The Challenge: Time Management

The **biggest struggle** for me has been **time management and motivation**. Unlike traditional **in-person programs** with strict deadlines and attendance policies, WGU's **self-paced structure** means you're **on your own**. 

Every WGU student is assigned a **program mentor**, but their level of involvement varies. Even the best mentors won't **micromanage your schedule**—**you have to stay disciplined and push yourself forward**.

## 🎯 Final Thoughts

This program is **ideal for independent learners** who want to **level up their careers**, but it demands **self-motivation and discipline**. If you're ready to **take control of your education** and **move up in IT**, this could be the perfect path for you!
